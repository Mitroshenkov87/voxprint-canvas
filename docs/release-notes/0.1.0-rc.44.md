# 0.1.0-rc.44

This release sits on `v0.1.0-rc.43`. The published `v0.1.0-rc.43` tag and its assets are unchanged. Build 42, still "Tavnit".

## Changes

- The generate-model picker lists only installed generate-group models, in two sections: "For 16 GB cards (compact)" and "Full size (24 GB+)". The section that matches the detected VRAM starts open and preselected; the other stays under "Show models for other cards". Optional plugins such as MOSS appear when they are installed. Encoders, VAEs, the writer, ControlNets, and the safety stack stay out of the picker.
- The installer catalog (root `mdl.json`) has 14 rows, including the two FP32 `-upstream` fallbacks and `mivolo-v2-age`. Every row has a label, a purpose, and a recognised task group (`generate`, `edit`, `writer`, or `safety`). The packaged `canvas/mdl.json` placeholder carries those same 14 ids plus two uncatalogued SVDQuant stubs (16 rows). SVDQuant is not in the installer catalog until it has real pins.
- voxprint-common 0.7.1 (commit `16804bf3`, wheel SHA-256 `7e5b1dd62ca1a4d298bc7fb610f03398929b231823151a312012d1d327b6f029`) is pinned in pyproject, constraints, CI, installers and smoke scripts. That wheel carries the optional `label`, `purpose`, and `group` fields on `Model`. An unrecognised group falls under "Other".
- GUI and CLI share one LoRA apply path. `apply_bound_lora` binds the selected LoRA and strength, loads the adapter, fuses it when possible, and writes one line with the LoRA id, alias, strength, base, and the number of modules patched (stderr and `voxprint.log`). `--lora` accepts the `voxprint-model.json` alias as well as the id and the path. `--save-metadata` is honoured on the parent CLI before the subcommand. The photoshoot overview contact sheet shows real thumbnails (PNG filters are unfiltered when the sheet is composed).
- When a Photoshoot series starts writing, a separate non-modal Photoshoot script window opens. Each row shows the prompt as written, the LoRA alias and strength, and whether the trigger was injected, and is linked to its kept picture. Closing the window does not stop the series; a Script (N) button in the Photoshoot area reopens the same window. Progress and Cancel stay in the Photoshoot area, so they still work when the pop-up is closed.
- When the subject is a person, the Photoshoot writer asks for a natural, anatomically plausible, stable pose. This is a quality rule only; the 18+ policy and the content gate are unchanged.
- `python -m canvas` launches the window the same way as `main.py`.

## Unchanged

- The content gate.
- The 18+ on/off policy.
- The published `v0.1.0-rc.43` tag.
